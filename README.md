# Privacy Policy for MyWineMap

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 28, 2026  

Welcome to **MyWineMap** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use MyWineMap, we may collect the following types of information:

* **Account & Profile Information:** If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication identifiers through Firebase Authentication (Google, Apple, or email/password sign-in).
* **Cellar & Tasting Notes Data:** Wine labels you scan, tasting notes, ratings, purchase prices, and cellar inventory details are saved to your account in secure cloud storage (Firebase Firestore) or stored locally on your device.
* **Camera & Photos Access:** With your explicit permission, MyWineMap accesses your device camera or photo library solely to allow you to photograph wine labels and bottle racks for AI identification and tasting journal creation. Photos are not used for any other purpose or sold to third parties.
* **Location Information (Optional):** With your permission, we may use your general geographic coordinates to display wine regions and nearby estates relevant to your location.
* **Usage & Diagnostics Data:** Anonymous technical telemetry (such as crash reports, app performance metrics, and feature usage) to improve app stability and user experience.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:
1. Providing core features: interactive 3D wine maps, cellar inventory tracking, portfolio valuation, and AI sommelier advice.
2. Syncing your personal tasting notes and cellar collection across your authorized devices.
3. Managing your subscription status and preventing unauthorized access or abuse.
4. Improving and maintaining application stability and performance.

We **NEVER** sell, rent, or trade your personal data or tasting records to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

MyWineMap integrates trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers:
* **Google Firebase & Google Cloud:** Cloud database (Firestore), authentication, and hosting infrastructure.
* **Apple In-App Purchases / StoreKit:** Subscription billing and transaction validation (we never store or process your credit card numbers).
* **OpenStreetMap & Map Providers:** Map tiles and geographic data visualization.

---

### 4. Data Security & Retention

We implement modern industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and secure cloud storage at rest. Your account and personal wine cellar data are retained as long as your account remains active.

---

### 5. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
* **Export & Review:** You can view and edit all your tasting notes and cellar bottles directly inside the app.
* **Account Deletion:** You may permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time from within the app settings or by contacting us at **support@mywinemap.app**. Upon deletion, your cloud data is permanently purged.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

MyWineMap is intended for adult audiences of legal drinking age in their respective jurisdictions (e.g., 21+ in the US). We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the legal drinking age.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
